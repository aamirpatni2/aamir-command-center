# Marketing workflow
1. Meta Ads access is read-only (`ads_read`) until explicitly upgraded. Any spend change is `financial` → owner-only approval.
2. Metrics are appended daily into `campaign_metrics` (unique campaign+date).
3. Ad copy follows Meta ad policies: no guaranteed income claims, no fake testimonials, no fake scarcity.
4. Audience ideas are labelled as hypotheses.
